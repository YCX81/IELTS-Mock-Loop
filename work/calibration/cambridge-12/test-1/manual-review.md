Each page was extracted from the PDF's single embedded scan image at its native pixel dimensions (approximately 1450 x 1930) and visually inspected in the conversation image channel.

## Confirmed layout and OCR repairs

- Pages 16-17 and 20-21 are two-column pages. Reading order is the complete left column followed by the complete right column; the old OCR interleaved lines from the two columns.
- Passage 1 uses British spelling and typography, including `specialised`, `aluminium`, `mould`, `centimetre`, `defence`, curly apostrophes, and en dashes.
- Passage 1 ends with plural subjects and verbs: cork forests `support` local biodiversity and `prevent` desertification.
- Page 20 reads `Similarly, in the 19th century,` and does not contain the OCR artefact `;19th`.
- Page 21 begins by completing the trainspotting sentence with `operations, or the technical specifications of different engine types.`
- The first question group on page 22 is headed only `Questions` in the printed book; it does not print `Questions 14-21`.
- Page 22 uses `Choose ONE WORD ONLY from the passage for each answer.`
- Passage 3 retains section labels A-F and its quoted course titles/terms; no decorative or full-page image is required in the lesson.
- Page 26 uses `Choose NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS from the passage for each answer.`
- The answer key writes `NOT GIVEN` with a space and gives the alternatives `contact/meetings`, `hunt/desire`, and `aimless/empty`.
