# 飞越群岛的陆龟：一场卡在时间与载荷边缘的放归

这篇文章从加拉帕戈斯陆龟如何适应群岛写起，再追踪人类捕猎、外来物种和栖息地破坏如何令其衰退。真正的转折在保护成功之后：繁育中心陆龟太多，放归又既不能太早也不能太晚，于是一次直升机运输把生态保护变成精密的时间、载荷与地面协作问题。

### A层深挖：give rise to

give rise to 在本句表示“产生、导致”，把岛屿间的环境差异写成14个亚种出现的因果来源。rise 原有“升起”画面，但整个固定表达不表示某物真的向上升；它比普通 cause 更正式，常用于结果逐渐形成且机制较复杂的学术语境。Cambridge Dictionary 的 [give rise to](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/give-rise-to) 条目把它释为“导致某事物产生”。在篇章中，这个表达完成从迁徙史到演化结果的关键转折。迁移：Unequal access to water can give rise to conflict.（用水机会不均可能引发冲突。）Long isolation gave rise to distinct dialects.（长期隔离产生了不同方言。）近义表达中，lead to 最中性；result in 强调最终结果；trigger 更强调突然触发。

中文：完成题为“The decline of the Galápagos tertoise”的笔记；每空只能从原文填写一个单词。注意原书标题把 tortoise 印作 tertoise，此处按原书保留。

### 第 8 题

**题干：** `17th century: small numbers taken onto ships used by 8 __________`

**翻译：** 17世纪：少量陆龟被带上由__________使用的船只

**官方答案：** `pirates`；可接受答案同为 `pirates`；字数限制：ONE WORD ONLY。

**原文证据：** `From the 17th century onwards, pirates took a few on board for food, but the arrival of whaling ships in the 1790s saw this exploitation grow exponentially.`

**定位逻辑：** Paragraph B, sentence 10。题干的17th century和small numbers锁定pirates took a few on board for food；food是目的，对应第9空，不可填本空。

### 第 9 题

**题干：** `1790s: very large numbers taken onto whaling ships, kept for 9 __________`

**翻译：** 1790年代：大量陆龟被带上捕鲸船，留作__________

**官方答案：** `food`；可接受答案同为 `food`；字数限制：ONE WORD ONLY。

**原文证据：** `Relatively immobile and capable of surviving for months without food or water, the tortoises were taken on board these ships to act as food supplies during long ocean passages.`

**定位逻辑：** Paragraph B, sentence 11。act as food supplies对应kept for food；water只是陆龟可以长期缺少的东西，不是保存目的。

### 第 10 题

**题干：** `and also used to produce 10 __________`

**翻译：** 还被用来生产__________

**官方答案：** `oil`；可接受答案同为 `oil`；字数限制：ONE WORD ONLY。

**原文证据：** `Sometimes, their bodies were processed into high-grade oil.`

**定位逻辑：** Paragraph B, sentence 12。bodies were processed into high-grade oil直接对应used to produce；high-grade是修饰语，ONE WORD ONLY只能填oil。

### 第 11 题

**题干：** `Hunted by 11 __________ on the islands`

**翻译：** 在岛上遭到__________捕猎

**官方答案：** `settlers`；可接受答案同为 `settlers`；字数限制：ONE WORD ONLY。

**原文证据：** `This historical exploitation was then exacerbated when settlers came to the islands. They hunted the tortoises and destroyed their habitat to clear land for agriculture.`

**定位逻辑：** Paragraph B, sentences 14–15。when settlers came后紧接They hunted the tortoises，They回指settlers；pirates和whalers在船只语境，不是岛上定居者。

### 第 12 题

**题干：** `Habitat destruction: for the establishment of agriculture and by various 12 __________ not native to the islands`

**翻译：** 栖息地破坏：为了发展农业，以及由各种并非岛屿原生的__________造成

**官方答案：** `species`；可接受答案同为 `species`；字数限制：ONE WORD ONLY。

**原文证据：** `They also introduced alien species – ranging from cattle, pigs, goats, rats and dogs to plants and ants – that either prey on the eggs and young tortoises or damage or destroy their habitat.`

**定位逻辑：** Paragraph B, sentence 16。alien species对应not native to the islands；cattle等只是例项，空格需上位名词species。

### 第 13 题

**题干：** `which also fed on baby tortoises and tortoises' 13 __________`

**翻译：** 这些生物还捕食幼龟和陆龟的__________

**官方答案：** `eggs`；可接受答案同为 `eggs`；字数限制：ONE WORD ONLY。

**原文证据：** `They also introduced alien species – ranging from cattle, pigs, goats, rats and dogs to plants and ants – that either prey on the eggs and young tortoises or damage or destroy their habitat.`

**定位逻辑：** Paragraph B, sentence 16。prey on the eggs and young tortoises与题干fed on baby tortoises and tortoises’ ... 语序互换；只能填eggs。
