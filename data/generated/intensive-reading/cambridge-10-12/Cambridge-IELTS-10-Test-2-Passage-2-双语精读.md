## 01 逐句精读
### 句 1（段落 A）

**原文：** Internationally, ‘giftedness’ is most frequently determined by a score on a general intelligence test, known as an IQ test, which is above a chosen cut-off point, usually at around the top 2–5%.

**翻译：** 在国际上，“天赋”最常通过一般智力测验（即 IQ 测验）的分数来判定；该分数须高于选定的分界点，通常位于最高的 2%–5% 左右。

**主干与修饰：** 主干是 giftedness is determined by a score。known as an IQ test 是过去分词短语补充说明 test；which 引导非限制性定语从句修饰 score；usually... 是对分界位置的补充。

**词汇与短语：** internationally (B2) 国际上；giftedness (C1，教育学专业词) 天赋；frequently (B1) 经常；determine (B1) 判定；general intelligence test (C1，心理测量专业词) 一般智力测验；be known as (B1) 被称为；chosen (B1) 选定的；cut-off point (C1) 分界点。

### 句 2（段落 A）

**原文：** Children’s educational environment contributes to the IQ score and the way intelligence is used.

**翻译：** 儿童所处的教育环境会影响其 IQ 分数以及智力的运用方式。

**主干与修饰：** 主干是 environment contributes to A and B；the way 后省略关系词 that/in which，说明“智力被运用的方式”。

**词汇与短语：** educational environment (B2) 教育环境；contribute to (B2) 促成、影响；IQ score (B2，心理测量专业词) 智商分数；intelligence (B2) 智力。

### 句 3（段落 A）

**原文：** For example, a very close positive relationship was found when children’s IQ scores were compared with their home educational provision (Freeman, 2010).

**翻译：** 例如，当研究者把儿童的 IQ 分数与其家庭教育条件进行比较时，发现二者存在非常密切的正相关关系（Freeman，2010）。

**主干与修饰：** 主干是 a relationship was found；when 引导时间兼研究条件从句；were compared with 是被动结构。句子用研究结果支撑上一句。

**词汇与短语：** for example 基础表达（A1/A2）；close relationship (B1) 密切关系；positive relationship (B2，统计学专业词) 正相关关系；compare with (B1) 与……比较；educational provision (C1) 教育条件、教育供给。

### 句 4（段落 A）

**原文：** The higher the children’s IQ scores, especially over IQ 130, the better the quality of their educational backup, measured in terms of reported verbal interactions with parents, number of books and activities in their home etc.

**翻译：** 儿童的 IQ 分数越高——尤其是超过 130 时——其教育支持的质量就越好；这种质量以报告的亲子言语互动、家中书籍和活动的数量等来衡量。

**主干与修饰：** The higher..., the better... 是“越……越……”比较级关联结构。measured... 是过去分词短语，说明 educational backup 的衡量指标。

**词汇与短语：** especially (B1) 尤其；quality (B1) 质量；educational backup (B2) 教育支持；measure in terms of (B2) 依据……衡量；reported (B2) 经报告的；verbal interaction (C1，教育学专业词) 言语互动；activity 基础词（A1/A2） 活动。

### 句 5（段落 A）

**原文：** Because IQ tests are decidedly influenced by what the child has learned, they are to some extent measures of current achievement based on age-norms; that is, how well the children have learned to manipulate their knowledge and know-how within the terms of the test.

**翻译：** 由于 IQ 测验显然会受到儿童已学内容的影响，因此它们在某种程度上衡量的是基于年龄常模的当前成就；也就是说，衡量儿童在测验规定的范围内运用知识和实际技能的熟练程度。

**主干与修饰：** Because 从句给原因，主句是 they are measures。based on... 修饰 achievement；分号后的 that is 对 measures 的含义作同位解释；how well 引导名词性从句。

**词汇与短语：** decidedly (C1) 明显地；influence (B2) 影响；to some extent (B2) 在某种程度上；current achievement (B2) 当前成就；age-norm (C1，心理测量专业词) 年龄常模；manipulate (C1) 熟练运用；know-how (B2) 实际技能；within the terms of (C1) 在……规定范围内。

### 句 6（段落 A）

**原文：** The vocabulary aspect, for example, is dependent on having heard those words.

**翻译：** 例如，词汇这一方面取决于儿童是否听过那些词。

**主干与修饰：** 主干是 aspect is dependent on...；having heard 是动名词完成式，强调此前已有的听词经历。

**词汇与短语：** vocabulary (B1) 词汇；aspect (B2) 方面；be dependent on (B2) 取决于；having heard (B2) 已经听过。

### 句 7（段落 A）

**原文：** But IQ tests can neither identify the processes of learning and thinking nor predict creativity.

**翻译：** 但 IQ 测验既不能识别学习与思考的过程，也不能预测创造力。

**主干与修饰：** neither...nor... 并列两个谓语 identify 与 predict，指出 IQ 测验的能力边界，转折收束 A 段。

**词汇与短语：** identify (B1) 识别；process (B1) 过程；predict (B1) 预测；creativity (B2) 创造力；neither...nor... (B2) 既不……也不……。

## A层深挖 1：研究中的“相关”不等于“单一因果”

A 段用 positive relationship 描述家庭教育条件与 IQ 分数的联系，却没有声称家庭资源是唯一原因。阅读题把 connection 改写为 relationship，把 domestic background 改写为 home educational provision。识别这种同义改写，比把每个名词机械对应更可靠。词义可核对 [relationship](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/relationship)。

### 句 8（段落 B）

**原文：** Excellence does not emerge without appropriate help.

**翻译：** 没有恰当的帮助，卓越便不会显现。

**主干与修饰：** 主干是 Excellence does not emerge；without 短语给出必要条件。短句承担段落主题句作用。

**词汇与短语：** excellence (C1) 卓越；emerge (B2) 显现；appropriate (B2) 恰当的。

### 句 9（段落 B）

**原文：** To reach an exceptionally high standard in any area very able children need the means to learn, which includes material to work with and focused challenging tuition – and the encouragement to follow their dream.

**翻译：** 要在任何领域达到异常高的水准，能力很强的儿童都需要学习所需的条件，其中包括可供使用的材料、聚焦而有挑战性的指导，以及追逐梦想的鼓励。

**主干与修饰：** To reach... 是目的状语；主干是 children need the means；which 引导非限制性定语从句，列举 means 的内容；破折号突出 encouragement。

**词汇与短语：** exceptionally (C1) 异常地；standard (B2) 水准；means (B2) 必要条件、手段；focused (B2) 聚焦的；challenging (B2) 有挑战性的；tuition (B2) 教学、指导；encouragement (B2) 鼓励；follow one’s dream (B2) 追逐梦想。

### 句 10（段落 B）

**原文：** There appears to be a qualitative difference in the way the intellectually highly able think, compared with more average-ability or older pupils, for whom external regulation by the teacher often compensates for lack of internal regulation.

**翻译：** 智力极高者的思考方式似乎存在质的差异；相比之下，对能力较为普通或年龄更大的学生而言，教师的外部调控往往会弥补其内部调控的不足。

**主干与修饰：** There appears to be... 是存在结构；the way 后为省略关系词的定语结构；compared with... 插入比较对象；for whom 引导非限制性定语从句修饰 pupils。

**词汇与短语：** There appears to be (B2) 似乎存在；compared with (B1) 与……相比；qualitative (C1) 质的；intellectually (C1) 在智力方面；average-ability (B2) 一般能力的；pupil (B1) 学生；external regulation (C1，教育心理学专业词) 外部调控；compensate for (C1) 弥补；internal regulation (C1，教育心理学专业词) 内部调控；lack of (B1) 缺乏。

### 句 11（段落 B）

**原文：** To be at their most effective in their self-regulation, all children can be helped to identify their own ways of learning – metacognition – which will include strategies of planning, monitoring, evaluation, and choice of what to learn.

**翻译：** 为了最有效地进行自我调控，可以帮助所有儿童识别自己的学习方式——即元认知——其中包括规划、监控、评估以及选择学习内容的策略。

**主干与修饰：** To be... 是目的状语；主句用被动 can be helped；metacognition 是同位语；which 从句解释其组成。

**词汇与短语：** effective (B1) 有效的；self-regulation (C1，教育心理学专业词) 自我调控；identify (B1) 识别；metacognition (C2，教育心理学专业词) 元认知；strategy (B2) 策略；planning (B1) 规划；choice (B1) 选择；monitoring (B2) 监控；evaluation (B2) 评估。

### 句 12（段落 B）

**原文：** Emotional awareness is also part of metacognition, so children should be helped to be aware of their feelings around the area to be learned, feelings of curiosity or confidence, for example.

**翻译：** 情绪意识也是元认知的一部分，因此应帮助儿童察觉自己面对待学领域时的感受，例如好奇或自信。

**主干与修饰：** so 连接因果并列句；to be learned 是后置定语修饰 area；末尾 feelings of... 同位补充说明 feelings。

**词汇与短语：** emotional awareness (C1，教育心理学专业词) 情绪意识；metacognition (C2，教育心理学专业词) 元认知；be aware of (B1) 意识到；curiosity (B2) 好奇心；confidence (B1) 自信。

### 句 13（段落 C）

**原文：** High achievers have been found to use self-regulatory learning strategies more often and more effectively than lower achievers, and are better able to transfer these strategies to deal with unfamiliar tasks.

**翻译：** 研究发现，高成就者比低成就者更频繁、也更有效地运用自我调控学习策略，而且更能迁移这些策略来处理陌生任务。

**主干与修饰：** have been found to use 是“被发现会……”；and 并列 are better able to transfer；than 引出比较对象；to deal with 表目的。

**词汇与短语：** high achiever (B2) 高成就者；self-regulatory (C1，教育心理学专业词) 自我调控的；effectively (B2) 有效地；be better able to (B2) 更有能力做……；transfer (B2) 迁移、转用；deal with (B1) 处理；unfamiliar (B2) 不熟悉的。

### 句 14（段落 C）

**原文：** This happens to such a high degree in some children that they appear to be demonstrating talent in particular areas.

**翻译：** 这种现象在一些儿童身上达到了如此高的程度，以至于他们似乎在特定领域展现出天赋。

**主干与修饰：** such...that... 构成结果结构；appear to be demonstrating 表示审慎推断，避免把表象写成绝对事实。

**词汇与短语：** to a high degree (B2) 达到很高程度；appear to (B1) 似乎；demonstrate (B2) 展现；talent (B1) 天赋；particular (B1) 特定的。

### 句 15（段落 C）

**原文：** Overviewing research on the thinking process of highly able children, (Shore and Kanevsky, 1993) put the instructor’s problem succinctly: ‘If they [the gifted] merely think more quickly, then we need only teach more quickly.

**翻译：** 在综览高能力儿童思维过程的研究后，Shore 和 Kanevsky（1993）简明地提出了教师面临的问题：“如果他们［天赋儿童］只不过是思考得更快，那么我们只需教得更快。”

**主干与修饰：** Overviewing... 是现在分词状语；主干是 Shore and Kanevsky put the problem succinctly；冒号后直接引语把第一种假设写成 if...then... 推论。

**词汇与短语：** overview (C1) 综览；thinking process (B2) 思维过程；highly able (B2) 高能力的；instructor (B2) 教师；succinctly (C2) 简明地；gifted (B2) 有天赋的；merely (B2) 仅仅；if...then... 基础表达（A1/A2）。

### 句 16（段落 C）

**原文：** If they merely make fewer errors, then we can shorten the practice’.

**翻译：** “如果他们只不过是犯更少的错误，那么我们就可以缩短练习。”

**主干与修饰：** if 从句提出条件，then 后主句给出推论；与上一句形成平行假设，也是第 18 题的直接证据。

**词汇与短语：** merely (B2) 仅仅；make an error (B1) 犯错；fewer (B1) 更少的；shorten (B2) 缩短；practice (B1) 练习。

### 句 17（段落 C）

**原文：** But of course, this is not entirely the case; adjustments have to be made in methods of learning and teaching, to take account of the many ways individuals think.

**翻译：** 但当然，事实并不完全如此；必须调整学习与教学方法，以顾及个体多样的思考方式。

**主干与修饰：** 分号连接判断与结论；adjustments have to be made 是被动必要结构；to take account of 表目的。

**词汇与短语：** entirely (B2) 完全地；adjustment (B2) 调整；method (B1) 方法；have to 基础表达（A1/A2）；take account of (C1) 考虑、顾及；individual (B1) 个体。

## A层深挖 2：作者为什么先引用、再否定简单推论

C 段先用 if...then... 展示“天赋儿童只是更快、更准”的简化模型，再用 not entirely the case 转向真正论点：教学法必须适应不同思维方式。第 18 题只问引语本身，因此仍应选 Shore and Kanevsky，而不能被后面的反驳带偏。关键词可核对 [merely](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/merely)。

### 句 18（段落 D）

**原文：** Yet in order to learn by themselves, the gifted do need some support from their teachers.

**翻译：** 然而，为了能够自主学习，天赋儿童确实需要教师给予一定支持。

**主干与修饰：** in order to 表目的；do need 是强调结构；some support 表明支持并非等于全面控制。

**词汇与短语：** in order to (B1) 为了；the gifted (B2) 天赋人群；do need (B1) 确实需要；support (B1) 支持。

### 句 19（段落 D）

**原文：** Conversely, teachers who have a tendency to ‘overdirect’ can diminish their gifted pupils’ learning autonomy.

**翻译：** 反过来说，有“过度指导”倾向的教师会削弱天赋学生的学习自主性。

**主干与修饰：** who 引导定语从句修饰 teachers；conversely 与上一句形成“需要支持但不能过度”的辩证关系。

**词汇与短语：** conversely (C1) 反过来说；have a tendency to (B2) 有……倾向；overdirect (C1，教育学专业词) 过度指导；diminish (C1) 削弱；autonomy (C1) 自主性；gifted (B2) 有天赋的。

### 句 20（段落 D）

**原文：** Although ‘spoon-feeding’ can produce extremely high examination results, these are not always followed by equally impressive life successes.

**翻译：** 虽然“填鸭式教学”可以带来极高的考试成绩，但这些成绩之后并不总会伴随同样令人瞩目的人生成就。

**主干与修饰：** Although 引导让步从句；these 回指 examination results；be followed by 表示后续结果。句子对比短期分数与长期成功。

**词汇与短语：** Although (B1) 虽然；be followed by (B2) 随后伴有；spoon-feeding (C1，教育学专业词) 填鸭式教学；produce (B1) 产生；extremely (B1) 极其；examination result (B1) 考试成绩；equally (B2) 同样地；impressive (B1) 令人印象深刻的；life success (B2) 人生成就。

### 句 21（段落 D）

**原文：** Too much dependence on the teacher risks loss of autonomy and motivation to discover.

**翻译：** 对教师依赖过多，会带来丧失自主性和探索动机的风险。

**主干与修饰：** 主干是 dependence risks loss；of autonomy and motivation 说明 loss 的内容；to discover 修饰 motivation。

**词汇与短语：** dependence (B2) 依赖；risk (B1) 使……面临风险；loss (B1) 丧失；autonomy (C1) 自主性；motivation (B2) 动机；discover (B1) 探索、发现。

### 句 22（段落 D）

**原文：** However, when teachers help pupils to reflect on their own learning and thinking activities, they increase their pupils’ self-regulation.

**翻译：** 然而，当教师帮助学生反思自己的学习和思维活动时，他们会增强学生的自我调控能力。

**主干与修饰：** when 引导条件兼时间从句；主句 they increase...；however 把“过度指导”转向“促进反思”的恰当支持。

**词汇与短语：** reflect on (B2) 反思；activity 基础词（A1/A2） 活动；increase (B1) 增强；self-regulation (C1，教育心理学专业词) 自我调控。

### 句 23（段落 D）

**原文：** For a young child, it may be just the simple question ‘What have you learned today?’ which helps them to recognise what they are doing.

**翻译：** 对年幼儿童而言，也许只需问一句简单的“你今天学到了什么？”，就能帮助他们意识到自己正在做什么。

**主干与修饰：** it 是强调性结构的先行形式；which 修饰 the simple question；what they are doing 是 recognise 的宾语从句。

**词汇与短语：** recognise (B1) 认识到；what they are doing 基础表达（A1/A2）；simple question 基础表达（A1/A2）。

### 句 24（段落 D）

**原文：** Given that a fundamental goal of education is to transfer the control of learning from teachers to pupils, improving pupils’ learning to learn techniques should be a major outcome of the school experience, especially for the highly competent.

**翻译：** 鉴于教育的一个根本目标是把学习控制权从教师转交给学生，提升学生“学会学习”的技巧应当成为学校经历的一项主要成果，尤其对能力很强的学生而言。

**主干与修饰：** Given that 引导原因背景；主句主语是动名词短语 improving...；transfer...from...to... 表控制权转移；especially... 补充适用重点。

**词汇与短语：** given that (C1) 鉴于；especially (B1) 尤其；learning to learn (C1，教育学专业表达) 学会学习；fundamental (B2) 根本的；transfer (B2) 转移；technique (B1) 技巧；major outcome (B2) 主要成果；school experience (B1) 学校经历；competent (B2) 能力强的。

### 句 25（段落 D）

**原文：** There are quite a number of new methods which can help, such as child-initiated learning, ability-peer tutoring, etc.

**翻译：** 有相当多的新方法可以发挥作用，例如儿童发起式学习、同等能力伙伴辅导等。

**主干与修饰：** There are 是存在句；which 引导定语从句修饰 methods；such as 引出课堂技术实例。

**词汇与短语：** quite a number of (B2) 相当多；method (B1) 方法；child-initiated learning (C1，教育学专业词) 儿童发起式学习；ability-peer tutoring (C1，教育学专业词) 同等能力伙伴辅导；tutoring (B2) 辅导。

### 句 26（段落 D）

**原文：** Such practices have been found to be particularly useful for bright children from deprived areas.

**翻译：** 研究发现，这些做法对来自贫困地区的聪慧儿童尤其有用。

**主干与修饰：** have been found to be 是被动研究表达；Such practices 回指上一句两种方法；from deprived areas 后置修饰 children。

**词汇与短语：** practice (B2) 做法；particularly (B1) 尤其；bright (B2) 聪慧的；deprived (C1) 贫困的、资源匮乏的；area (B1) 地区。

### 句 27（段落 E）

**原文：** But scientific progress is not all theoretical, knowledge is also vital to outstanding performance: individuals who know a great deal about a specific domain will achieve at a higher level than those who do not (Elshout, 1995).

**翻译：** 但科学进步并非全是理论层面的；知识对卓越表现也至关重要：对某一特定领域了解很多的人，会比不了解的人取得更高层次的成就（Elshout，1995）。

**主干与修饰：** 前两分句提出“理论之外还要知识”；冒号后解释；who 从句修饰 individuals；those who do not 省略重复的 know a great deal。

**词汇与短语：** scientific progress (B2) 科学进步；theoretical (B2) 理论的；vital (B2) 至关重要的；outstanding performance (B2) 卓越表现；a great deal (B2) 大量；specific domain (C1) 特定领域；achieve (B1) 取得成就。

### 句 28（段落 E）

**原文：** Research with creative scientists by Simonton (1988) brought him to the conclusion that above a certain high level, characteristics such as independence seemed to contribute more to reaching the highest levels of expertise than intellectual skills, due to the great demands of effort and time needed for learning and practice.

**翻译：** Simonton（1988）对创造型科学家的研究使他得出结论：达到某个较高水平之后，独立性等特征似乎比智力技能更有助于达到最高的专业造诣，因为学习和练习需要投入巨大的努力与时间。

**主干与修饰：** 主干是 Research brought him to the conclusion；that 引导同位内容；more...than... 比较两类因素；due to 引出原因；needed... 是过去分词短语修饰 effort and time。

**词汇与短语：** creative scientist (B2) 创造型科学家；seem to (B1) 似乎；effort (B1) 努力；bring someone to the conclusion (C1) 使某人得出结论；characteristic (B2) 特征；independence (B2) 独立性；contribute to (B2) 有助于；expertise (C1) 专业造诣；intellectual skill (B2) 智力技能；due to (B1) 由于；demand (B2) 要求、投入。

### 句 29（段落 E）

**原文：** Creativity in all forms can be seen as expertise mixed with a high level of motivation (Weisberg, 1993).

**翻译：** 各种形式的创造力都可以被视为专业能力与高度动机的结合（Weisberg，1993）。

**主干与修饰：** 主干是 Creativity can be seen as...；mixed with 是过去分词短语修饰 expertise，给创造力下概括性定义。

**词汇与短语：** creativity (B2) 创造力；in all forms (B2) 以各种形式；be seen as (B1) 被视为；expertise (C1) 专业能力；motivation (B2) 动机。

## A层深挖 3：support、overdirect 与 autonomy 的逻辑链

D 段不是反对教师支持，而是在区分“支架式支持”和“替学生控制学习”。适当提问可增强 self-regulation；过度指导则损害 autonomy，并可能让高分无法转化为长期成功。因此第 15、17、26 题虽然都定位 D 段，考查的却分别是损失、有效方法和填鸭式教学。核心词可核对 [autonomy](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/autonomy)。

### 句 30（段落 F）

**原文：** To sum up, learning is affected by emotions of both the individual and significant others.

**翻译：** 总而言之，学习会受到个体自身以及重要他人情绪的影响。

**主干与修饰：** 主干是 learning is affected；both...and... 并列 emotions 的两个来源；To sum up 标志全文总结。

**词汇与短语：** to sum up (B1) 总而言之；be affected by (B1) 受到……影响；individual (B1) 个体；significant others (C1，心理学专业词) 重要他人。

### 句 31（段落 F）

**原文：** Positive emotions facilitate the creative aspects of learning and negative emotions inhibit it.

**翻译：** 积极情绪会促进学习中的创造性方面，而消极情绪会抑制它。

**主干与修饰：** and 连接对照并列句；it 回指 the creative aspects of learning，而非笼统的全部学习。

**词汇与短语：** positive emotion (B2) 积极情绪；facilitate (C1) 促进；creative aspect (B2) 创造性方面；negative emotion (B2) 消极情绪；inhibit (C1) 抑制。

### 句 32（段落 F）

**原文：** Fear, for example, can limit the development of curiosity, which is a strong force in scientific advance, because it motivates problem-solving behaviour.

**翻译：** 例如，恐惧会限制好奇心的发展；好奇心是推动科学进步的一股强大力量，因为它能激发解决问题的行为。

**主干与修饰：** 主干是 Fear can limit...；which 非限制性定语从句修饰 curiosity；because 解释好奇心为何是推动力；it 回指 curiosity。

**词汇与短语：** fear 基础词（A1/A2）；limit (B1) 限制；development (B1) 发展；curiosity (B2) 好奇心；force (B2) 推动力；scientific advance (B2) 科学进步；motivate (B2) 激发；problem-solving behaviour (C1，心理学专业词) 问题解决行为。

### 句 33（段落 F）

**原文：** In Boekaerts’ (1991) review of emotion in the learning of very high IQ and highly achieving children, she found emotional forces in harness.

**翻译：** Boekaerts（1991）在评述超高 IQ 儿童和高成就儿童学习中的情绪时，发现他们能够驾驭情绪力量。

**主干与修饰：** 主干是 she found emotional forces in harness；句首介词短语交代研究来源；in harness 是比喻，表示力量受到控制并被利用。

**词汇与短语：** review (B2) 评述；highly achieving (B2) 高成就的；emotional force (B2) 情绪力量；in harness (C2) 受驾驭、受控制并被利用。

### 句 34（段落 F）

**原文：** They were not only curious, but often had a strong desire to control their environment, improve their learning efficiency, and increase their own learning resources.

**翻译：** 他们不仅好奇，而且常常强烈希望掌控自己的环境、提高学习效率并增加自身的学习资源。

**主干与修饰：** not only...but... 连接表语 curious 与谓语 had；to control、improve、increase 三个不定式并列修饰 desire。

**词汇与短语：** not only...but... (B1) 不仅……而且……；environment (B1) 环境；improve (B1) 改善、提高；curious (B1) 好奇的；desire (B2) 强烈愿望；control (B1) 控制；learning efficiency (B2) 学习效率；increase (B1) 增加；learning resources (B2) 学习资源。

### Questions 14–17：段落信息匹配

**题型指令：** Reading Passage 2 has six paragraphs, A–F. Which paragraph contains the following information? Write the correct letter, A–F, in boxes 14–17 on your answer sheet. NB You may use any letter more than once.

#### 14

**题干：** a reference to the influence of the domestic background on the gifted child
**翻译：** 提到家庭背景对天赋儿童的影响。
**官方答案：** A
**原文证据：** “a very close positive relationship ... children’s IQ scores ... home educational provision”
**定位逻辑：** domestic background 改写为 home educational provision，influence 改写为 positive relationship。
**干扰排除：** B、D 虽谈支持，却是教育或教师支持；只有 A 明确谈家庭环境。

#### 15

**题干：** reference to what can be lost if learners are given too much guidance
**翻译：** 提到学习者受到过多指导时可能失去什么。
**官方答案：** D
**原文证据：** “Too much dependence on the teacher risks loss of autonomy and motivation to discover.”
**定位逻辑：** too much guidance 对应 dependence on the teacher；what can be lost 对应 autonomy and motivation。
**干扰排除：** B 的 external regulation 用于解释能力差异，D 才明确出现 risks loss。

#### 16

**题干：** a reference to the damaging effects of anxiety
**翻译：** 提到焦虑的破坏性影响。
**官方答案：** F
**原文证据：** “Fear ... can limit the development of curiosity”
**定位逻辑：** anxiety 与 fear 同义，damaging effects 对应 limit the development。
**干扰排除：** 其他段讨论认知或教学，F 才明确说明负面情绪限制好奇心。

#### 17

**题干：** examples of classroom techniques which favour socially-disadvantaged children
**翻译：** 有利于社会弱势儿童的课堂技巧实例。
**官方答案：** D
**原文证据：** “child-initiated learning, ability-peer tutoring ... particularly useful for bright children from deprived areas.”
**定位逻辑：** techniques 对应 methods，socially-disadvantaged 对应 deprived areas。
**干扰排除：** B、C 是原则或研究结论，没有同时出现具体课堂方法与弱势儿童。

#### 18

**题干：** Less time can be spent on exercises with gifted pupils who produce accurate work.
**翻译：** 对作业准确的天赋学生，可以减少练习时间。
**官方答案：** B — Shore and Kanevsky

**定位逻辑：** accurate work = fewer errors；less time = shorten the practice。
**干扰排除：** Elshout 讲学科知识；Simonton 讲独立性；Boekaerts 讲情绪。

#### 19

**题干：** Self-reliance is a valuable tool that helps gifted students reach their goals.
**翻译：** 自立是帮助天赋学生实现目标的宝贵工具。
**官方答案：** D — Simonton
**原文证据：** “independence seemed to contribute more to reaching the highest levels of expertise”.
**定位逻辑：** self-reliance = independence；reach goals = reach the highest levels。
**干扰排除：** Elshout 强调知识量，不是独立性；Freeman 强调家庭条件。

#### 20

**题干：** Gifted children know how to channel their feelings to assist their learning.
**翻译：** 天赋儿童懂得如何引导情绪来帮助学习。
**官方答案：** E — Boekaerts

**定位逻辑：** channel their feelings 对应 emotional forces in harness。
**干扰排除：** Simonton 的 independence 不是情绪管理；Shore and Kanevsky 讨论教学调整。

#### 21

**官方答案：** A — Freeman

**定位逻辑：** very gifted = over IQ 130；close relatives = parents；support = educational backup。
**干扰排除：** D 段教师支持不是 close relatives；其他研究者未研究家庭资源。

**题干：** Really successful students have learnt a considerable amount about their subject.

**题型指令：** Complete the sentences below. Choose NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS from the passage for each answer. Write your answers in boxes 23–26 on your answer sheet.

#### 23

**题干：** One study found a strong connection between children’s IQ and the availability of ______ and ______ at home.
**翻译：** 一项研究发现，儿童 IQ 与家中可获得的 ______ 和 ______ 之间存在紧密联系。

IN EITHER ORDER; BOTH REQUIRED
FOR ONE MARK
books (and)
activities

#### 24

**题干：** Children of average ability seem to need more direction from teachers because they do not have ______.

**可接受答案：** internal regulation；self-regulation。
**原文证据：** “external regulation by the teacher often compensates for lack of internal regulation.”
**定位逻辑：** more direction = external regulation；do not have = lack of。两个官方变体均不超过两词。
**干扰排除：** metacognition 是后一句的广义概念，不是此处与 external regulation 对举的词。

#### 25
