# LAND OF THE RISING SUN｜日出之国

这篇文章并不是简单赞美日本学生“更聪明”，而是在比较英日数学成绩后，把镜头移进日本初中课堂：统一课程与教材、固定课堂流程、学生自我订正、混合能力编班、同伴与家庭支持，共同构成了成绩差异。作者最后也修正了一个常见成见：重复讲解并不必然意味着课堂枯燥，关键在于努力、准确性和互助机制如何结合。

## 逐句精读

### A

#### 1

**Japan has a significantly better record in terms of average mathematical attainment than England and Wales.**

日本在平均数学成绩方面的表现明显优于英格兰和威尔士。

主干是 `Japan has a ... better record than ...`；`in terms of` 限定比较维度。`significantly` **B2**，显著地；`have a better record` **B2**，表现或往绩更好；`in terms of` **B2**，就……而言；`average` **B1**，平均的；`mathematical` **B2**，数学的；`attainment` **C1，教育领域**，学业成就、达到的水平。

**A层深挖 1：attainment。** 本句指经过学习后达到的数学水平。它由 `attain`（达到）加名词后缀 `-ment` 构成，常见搭配有 `educational/academic attainment`。它比普通的 `result` 更强调“达到的能力或成就水平”，语域正式，常用于教育研究，不宜把一次小测分数都称为 attainment。这里它把讨论从个别高分转向整体教育成果，为全文的比较研究定调。[Cambridge Dictionary: attainment](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/attainment)。例：`Early reading support can improve educational attainment.` 早期阅读支持可以提高教育成就。`The report compares attainment across age groups.` 该报告比较不同年龄组达到的学业水平。近义表达中，`achievement` 范围更广，强调取得的成果；`performance` 强调某次任务中的表现；`score` 只指具体分数。

#### 2

**Large sample international comparisons of pupils’ attainments since the 1960s have established that not only did Japanese pupils at age 13 have better scores of average attainment, but there was also a larger proportion of ‘low’ attainers in England, where, incidentally, the variation in attainment scores was much greater.**

自20世纪60年代以来，大样本国际比较已经证实：13岁的日本学生不仅平均成绩更好，而且英格兰的“低成就者”比例更高；顺带一提，英格兰成绩的差异幅度也大得多。

主干是 `comparisons have established that ...`；宾语从句采用 `not only ... but also ...`，前半句因 `not only` 前置而部分倒装；`where` 引出补充说明英格兰的非限制性定语从句。`large sample` **B2，研究领域**，大样本；`international comparison` **B2**，国际比较；`pupil` **B1**，本句出现两次，均指学生；`attainment` **C1，教育领域**，本句三次出现，分别在复数所有格、平均水平和成绩差异中；`establish that` **B2**，证实；`score` **B1**，本句出现两次，均指分数；`proportion` **B2**，比例；`attainer` **C1，教育领域**，达到某水平者；`incidentally` **B2**，顺带一提；`variation` **B2**，差异、变动幅度；`not only ... but also ...` **B1**，不仅……而且……；`much greater` **B1**，大得多。

#### 3

**The percentage of Gross National Product spent on education is reasonably similar in the two countries, so how is this higher and more consistent attainment in maths achieved?**

两国国民生产总值中用于教育的比例相当接近，那么日本这种更高、更稳定的数学水平究竟是怎样取得的呢？

`spent on education` 是过去分词短语，修饰 `Gross National Product`；`so` 把数据转为全文核心问题。`percentage` **B1**，百分比；`Gross National Product` **C1，经济领域**，国民生产总值；`reasonably` **B2**，相当地；`similar` **B1**，相似的；`consistent` **B2**，稳定一致的；`attainment` **C1，教育领域**，再次出现；`achieve` **B1**，取得、实现。

### B

#### 4

**Lower secondary schools in Japan cover three school years, from the seventh grade (age 13) to the ninth grade (age 15).**

日本的初中涵盖三个学年，从七年级（13岁）到九年级（15岁）。

`from ... to ...` 标出范围。`lower secondary school` **B2，教育领域**，初中；`cover` **B1**，涵盖；`grade` 在这里指年级，是**基础词/基础表达**。

#### 5

**Virtually all pupils at this stage attend state schools: only 3 per cent are in the private sector.**

这一阶段几乎所有学生都就读公立学校，只有3%属于私立教育部门。

冒号后的数字解释 `virtually all`。`virtually` **B2**，几乎；`at this stage` **B2**，在这一阶段；`attend` **B1**，就读；`state school` **B1，教育领域**，公立学校；`private sector` **B2**，私营部门，此处指私立教育。

#### 6

**Schools are usually modern in design, set well back from the road and spacious inside.**

学校的设计通常很现代，建筑远离道路，内部也很宽敞。

`set ... back from` 是过去分词结构，和 `spacious` 并列描述校舍。`modern in design` **B1**，设计现代；`set well back from` **B2**，远离……设置；`spacious` **B2**，宽敞的。

#### 7

**Classrooms are large and pupils sit at single desks in rows.**

教室很大，学生一人一桌，成排就座。

`single desks` 指单人课桌。`classroom` 是**基础词/基础表达**，教室；`pupil` **B1**，再次出现；`in rows` **B1**，成排地。

#### 8

**Lessons last for a standardised 50 minutes and are always followed by a 10-minute break, which gives the pupils a chance to let off steam.**

每节课按统一标准持续50分钟，之后总有10分钟休息，让学生有机会释放精力。

`which` 指代整个休息安排。`last for` **B1**，持续；`standardised` **C1，教育领域**，标准化的；`be followed by` **B1**，随后是；`break` 在这里指课间休息，是**基础词/基础表达**；`pupil` **B1**，本篇再次出现；`give someone a chance to` **B1**，给某人机会做……；`let off steam` **C1**，释放压力或过剩精力。

**A层深挖 2：let off steam。** 本句并非“放出蒸汽”的字面动作，而是说学生在课间释放积压的精力。这个表达来自压力容器排出蒸汽的画面，常与 `need to`、`a chance to` 搭配。它比 `relax` 更有“把憋着的情绪或能量发泄出来”的动态感，适合非正式和中性语境；若涉及攻击性行为，要谨慎使用。文章借它说明严格课堂与必要休息并存，使日本课堂并非只有纪律。[Cambridge Dictionary: let off steam](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/let-off-steam)。例：`A short walk helps me let off steam after a difficult meeting.` 艰难会议后，散步一会儿能让我释放压力。`The school gives children space to let off steam between lessons.` 学校让孩子们在课间有空间活动放松。相近表达中，`unwind` 强调逐渐放松；`vent` 常指说出负面情绪；`blow off steam` 与本表达近义，但更常见于美式英语。

#### 9

**Teachers begin with a formal address and mutual bowing, and then concentrate on whole-class teaching.**

教师先进行正式致意并彼此鞠躬，随后集中开展全班教学。

`begin with ... and then ...` 展示课堂顺序。`formal address` **B2**，正式致意；`mutual` **B2**，相互的；`bowing` **B2，文化行为**，鞠躬；`concentrate on` **B1**，专注于；`whole-class teaching` **B2，教育领域**，全班教学。

#### 10

**Classes are large – usually about 40 – and are unstreamed.**

班级规模很大，通常约40人，而且不按能力分班。

破折号插入具体人数。`unstreamed` **C1，教育领域**，未按能力分流的；`class` 表示班级，在此为**基础词**。

#### 11

**Pupils stay in the same class for all lessons throughout the school and develop considerable class identity and loyalty.**

学生在整个学校阶段的所有课程中都留在同一个班，因此形成很强的班级认同感和忠诚感。

`stay ... throughout ...` 描述持续状态。`pupil` **B1**，本篇再次出现；`throughout` **B2**，贯穿；`develop` **B1**，形成；`considerable` **B2**，相当大的；`class identity` **B2，教育/社会领域**，班级认同；`loyalty` **B2**，忠诚感。

#### 12

**Pupils attend the school in their own neighbourhood, which in theory removes ranking by school.**

学生在自己居住的社区就近入学，理论上这消除了学校之间的等级排序。

`which` 指代就近入学制度。`pupil` **B1**，本篇再次出现；`attend` **B1**，本篇再次出现；`neighbourhood` **B1**，社区；`in theory` **B2**，理论上；`remove` **B1**，消除；`ranking` **B2，教育领域**，排名、等级排序。

#### 13

**In practice in Tokyo, because of the relative concentration of schools, there is some competition to get into the ‘better’ school in a particular area.**

但在东京的实际情况中，由于学校相对集中，人们仍会竞争进入某一地区所谓“更好”的学校。

`in practice` 与上一句 `in theory` 对照。`in practice` **B2**，实际上；`relative` **B2**，相对的；`concentration` **B2**，集中；`competition` **B1**，竞争；`get into` **B1**，获准进入；`particular` **B1**，特定的。引号中的 `better` 暗示这种等级是社会认知，而非作者直接认可。

### C

#### 14

**Traditional ways of teaching form the basis of the lesson and the remarkably quiet classes take their own notes of the points made and the examples demonstrated.**

传统教学方式构成课堂基础；异常安静的学生们自行记录教师讲解的要点和演示的例题。

两个并列谓语是 `form` 与 `take`；`made`、`demonstrated` 均为过去分词后置修饰。`traditional` **B1**，传统的；`form the basis of` **B2**，构成……的基础；`remarkably` **B2**，格外地；`take notes of` **B1**，记录；`point` **B1**，要点；`demonstrate` **B2**，演示、示范。

#### 15

**Everyone has their own copy of the textbook supplied by the central education authority, Monbusho, as part of the concept of free compulsory education up to the age of 15.**

每个人都有一本由中央教育主管机构文部省提供的教材，这是15岁以前免费义务教育理念的一部分。

`supplied by ...` 修饰 `textbook`；`as part of` 说明制度归属。`textbook` **B1，教育领域**，教材；`supply` **B1**，提供；`central education authority` **B2，教育领域**，中央教育主管机构；`as part of` **B1**，作为……的一部分；`concept` **B2**，理念；`compulsory education` **B2，教育领域**，义务教育；`up to the age of` **B1**，直到……岁。

#### 16

**These textbooks are, on the whole, small, presumably inexpensive to produce, but well set out and logically developed.**

总体而言，这些教材篇幅不大，制作成本大概也不高，但编排清楚，内容发展合乎逻辑。

`but` 把外形和成本的朴素与内容质量形成对照。`textbook` **B1，教育领域**，本篇再次出现；`on the whole` **B2**，总体上；`presumably` **B2**，大概、据推测；`inexpensive` **B1**，不昂贵的；`produce` **B1**，制作；`well set out` **B2**，编排清楚；`logically` **B2**，合乎逻辑地；`developed` **B2**，展开、编排成熟的。

#### 17

**(One teacher was particularly keen to introduce colour and pictures into maths textbooks: he felt this would make them more accessible to pupils brought up in a cartoon culture.)**

（一位教师尤其希望把色彩和图片引入数学教材：他认为这会让在漫画文化中长大的学生更容易理解教材。）

括号表示补充观察；`brought up ...` 修饰 `pupils`。`particularly` **B1**，尤其；`be keen to` **B1**，热衷于、很想；`introduce ... into ...` **B2**，把……引入……；`maths textbook` **B1，教育领域**，数学教材；`accessible to` **B2**，对……容易理解或使用；`pupil` **B1**，本篇再次出现；`bring up` **B1**，养育；`cartoon culture` **B2，文化领域**，漫画文化。

#### 18

**Besides approving textbooks, Monbusho also decides the highly centralised national curriculum and how it is to be delivered.**

除审批教材外，文部省还决定高度集中的国家课程以及课程应如何实施。

`Besides + -ing` 补充职责；`how it is to be delivered` 是并列宾语。`besides` **B1**，除……之外还；`approve` **B2**，审批；`textbook` **B1，教育领域**，本篇再次出现；`highly centralised` **C1，治理/教育领域**，高度集中的；`national curriculum` **C1，教育领域**，国家课程；`deliver a curriculum` **C1，教育领域**，实施课程。

### D

#### 19

**Lessons all follow the same pattern.**

所有课程都遵循同一种模式。

`follow a pattern` **B1**，遵循某种模式；`pattern` **B1**，模式。

#### 20

**At the beginning, the pupils put solutions to the homework on the board, then the teachers comment, correct or elaborate as necessary.**

开始时，学生把家庭作业的解答写在黑板上，然后教师根据需要进行点评、纠正或进一步讲解。

`as necessary` 修饰三个并列动词。`pupil` **B1**，本篇再次出现；`solution` **B1**，解答；`comment` **B1**，评论；`correct` **B1**，纠正；`elaborate` **C1**，详细说明；`as necessary` **B2**，视需要而定。

#### 21

**Pupils mark their own homework: this is an important principle in Japanese schooling as it enables pupils to see where and why they made a mistake, so that these can be avoided in future.**

学生自己批改作业：这是日本学校教育的一项重要原则，因为它让学生看清自己在哪里、为什么犯错，从而以后避免这些错误。

冒号解释做法的教育意义；`as` 表原因，`so that` 表结果。`pupil` **B1**，本句出现两次；`mark homework` **B1，教育领域**，批改作业；`principle` **B2**，原则；`schooling` **C1，教育领域**，学校教育；`enable someone to` **B2**，使某人能够；`avoid` **B1**，避免；`in future` **B1**，今后。

#### 22

**No one minds mistakes or ignorance as long as you are prepared to learn from them.**

只要你愿意从错误和无知中学习，就没有人介意你犯错或有所不知。

`as long as` 给出容许错误的条件。`mind` **B1**，介意；`ignorance` **C1**，无知、不了解；`as long as` **B1**，只要；`be prepared to` **B1**，愿意并准备做；`learn from` **B1**，从……中学习。

#### 23

**After the homework has been discussed, the teacher explains the topic of the lesson, slowly and with a lot of repetition and elaboration.**

讨论完作业后，教师会缓慢地讲解本课主题，并进行大量重复和扩展说明。

现在完成时被动强调作业讨论先完成。`discuss` **B1**，讨论；`topic` **B1**，主题；`repetition` **C1**，重复；`elaboration` **C1**，详尽说明，本词与第20句 `elaborate` 同族但仍是一次新的出现。

#### 24

**Examples are demonstrated on the board; questions from the textbook are worked through first with the class, and then the class is set questions from the textbook to do individually.**

例题先在黑板上演示；教材中的问题先由全班共同逐步完成，然后教师再布置教材题目，让学生独立作答。

分号连接同一教学流程；两个被动结构突出步骤。`demonstrate` **B2**，再次出现；`textbook` **B1，教育领域**，本句出现两次；`work through` **B2**，逐步处理直至完成；`set someone questions` **B2，教育领域**，给某人布置题目；`individually` **B1**，单独地。

#### 25

**Only rarely are supplementary worksheets distributed in a maths class.**

数学课上极少发放补充练习单。

否定意义副词 `Only rarely` 前置引发部分倒装，突出“极少”。`rarely` **B1**，很少；`supplementary` **C1，教育领域**，补充性的；`worksheet` **B2，教育领域**，练习单；`distribute` **B2**，分发。

#### 26

**The impression is that the logical nature of the textbooks and their comprehensive coverage of different types of examples, combined with the relative homogeneity of the class, renders work sheets unnecessary.**

给人的印象是：教材合乎逻辑的性质及其对不同例题类型的全面覆盖，再加上班级相对同质，使练习单显得没有必要。

主干为 `The impression is that A renders B unnecessary`；`combined with ...` 补充原因。`impression` **B1**，印象；`logical nature` **B2**，逻辑性；`textbook` **B1，教育领域**，本篇再次出现；`comprehensive coverage` **C1**，全面覆盖；`combine with` **B1**，与……结合；`relative` **B2**，再次出现；`homogeneity` **C2，教育/社会研究领域**，同质性；`render` **C1**，使成为；`work sheet` **B2，教育领域**，练习单；`unnecessary` **B1**，不必要的。原书此处写作两词 `work sheets`，应保留。

#### 27

**At this point, the teacher would circulate and make sure that all the pupils were coping well.**

此时，教师会在教室里巡视，确保所有学生都能顺利应付学习任务。

`would` 描述观察到的惯常行为。`at this point` **B1**，此时；`circulate` **C1**，走动、巡视；`make sure` **B1**，确保；`pupil` **B1**，本篇再次出现；`cope` **B2**，应付、处理。

### E

#### 28

**It is remarkable that large, mixed-ability classes could be kept together for maths throughout all their compulsory schooling from 6 to 15.**

#### 29

**Teachers say that they give individual help at the end of a lesson or after school, setting extra work if necessary.**

教师说，他们会在课末或放学后提供个别帮助，并在必要时布置额外任务。

#### 30

**In observed lessons, any strugglers would be assisted by the teacher or quietly seek help from their neighbour.**

#### 31

**Carefully fostered class identity makes pupils keen to help each other – anyway, it is in their interests since the class progresses together.**

精心培养的班级认同使学生乐于互相帮助；不管怎样，这也符合他们自身利益，因为全班共同进步。

#### 32

**This scarcely seems adequate help to enable slow learners to keep up.**

学校如何帮助遇到学习困难的学生？

`deal with` **B1**，处理；`experience difficulties` **B2**，遇到困难。

- **A They are given appropriate supplementary tuition.**｜他们会得到适当的补充辅导。`appropriate` **B2**，合适的；`supplementary tuition` **C1，教育领域**，补充辅导。
- **B They are encouraged to copy from other pupils.**｜他们被鼓励抄其他学生的答案。`encourage` **B1**，再次出现；`copy from` **B1**，抄自；`pupil` **B1**，再次出现。
- **C They are forced to explain their slow progress.**｜他们被迫解释自己进步缓慢的原因。`be forced to` **B1**，被迫；`progress` **B1**，第三次出现。
- **D They are placed in a mixed-ability class.**｜他们被安排进混合能力班。`be placed in` **B1**，被安排在；`mixed-ability class` **C1，教育领域**，再次出现。

**官方答案：A。**

**连续证据原文：** Parents are kept closely informed of their children’s progress and will play a part in helping their children to keep up with class, sending them to ‘Juku’ (private evening tuition) if extra help is needed and encouraging them to work harder.

需要额外帮助的学生会接受私人晚间补习，正是 `appropriate supplementary tuition`。

**正式干扰项（3项）：** B 邻座可以帮助，但原文从未鼓励抄袭；C 没有学生被迫解释进步缓慢；D 所有班本来就是混合能力班，编班本身不是针对困难学生的补救措施。

### 第13题

**题干：** Why do Japanese students tend to achieve relatively high rates of success in maths?  
为什么日本学生往往能在数学上取得相对较高的成功率？

`tend to` **B1**，往往；`achieve` **B1**，再次出现；`relatively` **B2**，相对地；`rate of success` **B2**，成功率。

- **A It is a compulsory subject in Japan.**｜数学在日本是必修科目。`compulsory subject` **B2，教育领域**，再次出现。
- **B They are used to working without help from others.**｜他们习惯于在没有他人帮助的情况下学习。`be used to doing` **B1**，习惯做；`without help from` **B1**，没有……帮助。
- **C Much effort is made and correct answers are emphasised.**｜学生付出大量努力，正确答案也受到强调。`effort` **B1**，努力；`emphasise` **B2**，强调。
- **D There is a strong emphasis on repetitive learning.**｜教学强烈强调重复学习。`strong emphasis on` **B2**，强烈强调；`repetitive` **C1**，重复性的。

**官方答案：C。**

**连续证据原文：** Education is valued greatly in Japanese culture; maths is recognised as an important compulsory subject throughout schooling; and the emphasis is on hard work coupled with a focus on accuracy.

`hard work` 对应 `much effort`，`a focus on accuracy` 对应强调正确答案，因此C完整概括两个关键因素。

**正式干扰项（3项）：** A 必修地位只是因素之一，不能完整回答；B 原文强调同伴、教师和家长帮助，和“无需他人帮助”相反；D 作者认为“重复而枯燥”的刻板看法可能不公平且无依据。
