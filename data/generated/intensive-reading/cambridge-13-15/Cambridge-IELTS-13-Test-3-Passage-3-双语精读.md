# Cambridge IELTS 13 Test 3 Reading Passage 3 双语精读

## Whatever happened to the Harappan Civilisation?

### New research sheds light on the disappearance of an ancient society

这篇文章追问哈拉帕文明为何从繁荣走向消失。作者没有把单一气候事件写成定论，而是先梳理旧假说，再展示地点校正、湖泊沉积物、谷物和陶器等新证据，最后把古代社会应对环境压力的经验带回今天的公共治理。

## 正文逐句精读

### 1

> The Harappan Civilisation of ancient Pakistan and India flourished 5,000 years ago, but a thousand years later their cities were abandoned.

古代巴基斯坦和印度的哈拉帕文明在五千年前繁荣兴盛，但一千年后，其城市遭到废弃。

主干由 `The ... Civilisation flourished` 与 `their cities were abandoned` 并列，but 把繁荣与废弃正面相撞；两个时间点迅速建立全文谜题。

本句词汇与短语：`Harappan Civilisation`（C1，考古学专业表达）哈拉帕文明；`ancient`（B1）古代的；`flourish`（C2）繁荣；`civilisation`（B2）文明；`abandon`（B2）遗弃、废弃。

### 2

> The Harappan Civilisation was a sophisticated Bronze Age society who built ‘megacities’ and traded internationally in luxury craft products, and yet seemed to have left almost no depictions of themselves.

哈拉帕文明是一个先进复杂的青铜时代社会，建造了“巨型城市”，并进行奢侈手工制品的国际贸易，然而似乎几乎没有留下自我形象。

主干是 `The ... Civilisation was a ... society`；who 从句以 `built` 和 `traded` 并列写成就，`and yet` 转向反常缺失，`seemed to have left` 用完成不定式谨慎推断过去结果。

本句词汇与短语：`Harappan Civilisation`（C1，考古学专业表达）哈拉帕文明；`sophisticated`（C1）先进复杂的；`Bronze Age`（B2，考古学专业词）青铜时代；`society`（B1）社会；`megacity`（C1，城市研究专业词）巨型城市；`trade internationally`（B2，商业专业表达）开展国际贸易；`luxury`（B2）奢侈的；`craft product`（B2）手工制品；`and yet`（B2）然而；`seem to have done`（B2）似乎已经做过；`depiction`（C2）描绘、形象。

### 3

> But their lack of self-imagery – at a time when the Egyptians were carving and painting representations of themselves all over their temples – is only part of the mystery.

但他们缺乏自我图像——而同一时期，埃及人正在各处神庙雕刻、绘制自己的形象——这只是谜团的一部分。

主干是 `their lack ... is only part of the mystery`；破折号插入 when 从句形成同代文明对比。对比说明“没有留下形象”不是时代共性，而是哈拉帕文明的特殊现象。

本句词汇与短语：`lack of`（B1）缺乏；`self-imagery`（C2，艺术史专业词）自我图像；`Egyptian`（B1，历史专名）埃及人；`carve`（C1）雕刻；`representation`（C2）形象、表现；`temple`（B1）神庙；`mystery`（B1）谜团。

### 4

> ‘There is plenty of archaeological evidence to tell us about the rise of the Harappan Civilisation, but relatively little about its fall,’ explains archaeologist Dr Cameron Petrie of the University of Cambridge.

“有大量考古证据告诉我们哈拉帕文明如何兴起，但关于它如何衰落的证据相对很少。”剑桥大学考古学家Cameron Petrie博士解释道。

引语主干是存在句 `There is plenty of ... evidence`，but 后省略重复的 evidence，形成 rise/fall 对照；引语后的倒装式报告语标明说话者。本句给研究必要性。

本句词汇与短语：`plenty of`（B1）大量；`archaeological evidence`（C1，考古学专业表达）考古证据；`rise`（B2）兴起；`Harappan Civilisation`（C1，考古学专业表达）哈拉帕文明；`relatively`（B2）相对地；`fall`（B2）衰落；`archaeologist`（C1，考古学专业词）考古学家；`explain`：基础词（A1/A2），解释。

### 5

> ‘As populations increased, cities were built that had great baths, craft workshops, palaces and halls laid out in distinct sectors.

“随着人口增长，人们建起城市；城内的大型浴场、手工作坊、宫殿和大厅分布在不同区域。

As 从句交代伴随背景；主干是被动句 `cities were built`，that 从句列举城市设施，`laid out in distinct sectors` 描述所列浴场、作坊、宫殿和大厅整体按不同区域规划布置的状态。细节将“繁荣”落到城市设计。

本句词汇与短语：`population`（B1）人口；`increase`（B1）增长；`craft workshop`（B2）手工作坊；`palace`（B1）宫殿；`hall`（B1）大厅；`lay out`（B2）规划、布置；`distinct`（B2）不同的、清晰分开的；`sector`（B2，城市规划专业词）区域。

### 6

> Houses were arranged in blocks, with wide main streets and narrow alleyways, and many had their own wells and drainage systems.

房屋按街区排列，设有宽阔的主街和狭窄的小巷，许多房屋还有自己的水井和排水系统。

前半主干是被动句 `Houses were arranged`，with 短语补充街道格局；and 后 `many` 回指 houses，列出供水排水设施。句子从公共建筑转向居住单元。

本句词汇与短语：`arrange`（B1）安排、排列；`block`（B1，城市规划专业词）街区；`main street`（B1）主街；`narrow`（B1）狭窄的；`alleyway`（C1）小巷；`well`：基础词（A1/A2），水井；`drainage system`（C1，工程专业表达）排水系统。

### 7

> It was very much a “thriving” civilisation.’

“这的确是一个‘兴旺发达’的文明。”

主干是系表结构 `It was a ... civilisation`，`very much` 强化判断；引号中的 thriving 既概括前两句，也与随后衰败形成落差。

本句词汇与短语：`very much`（B1）确实、非常；`thriving`（C1）兴旺的；`civilisation`（B2）文明。

### 8

> Then around 2100 BC, a transformation began.

随后，大约在公元前2100年，一场转变开始了。

主干极短：`a transformation began`；时间状语把静态繁荣切换为衰落过程，承担段落转轴作用。

本句词汇与短语：`BC`（B1，历史纪年表达）公元前；`transformation`（B2）转变。

### 9

> Streets went uncleaned, buildings started to be abandoned, and ritual structures fell out of use.

街道无人清扫，建筑开始遭到废弃，礼仪性建筑也停止使用。

三个并列分句按“维护停止—建筑废弃—仪式中断”递进；`go + 过去分词` 写状态变化，`fall out of use` 写功能逐渐消失。它描述的是衰落迹象，不是原因。

本句词汇与短语：`uncleaned`（C1）未清扫的；`abandon`（B2）废弃；`ritual`（C1，宗教专业词）仪式的；`structure`（B2）建筑物、结构；`fall out of use`（C1）停止使用。

### 10

> After their final demise, a millennium passed before really large-scale cities appeared once more in South Asia.

在它们最终消亡之后，过了一千年，南亚才再次出现真正的大型城市。

主干是 `a millennium passed`，before 从句说明等待到何时结束；`once more` 回扣此前的巨型城市。千年空档强化文明崩溃的规模。

本句词汇与短语：`final demise`（C2）最终消亡；`millennium`（C1）一千年；`large-scale`（B2）大规模的；`appear`（B1）出现；`once more`（B1）再次；`pass`（B1）经过、过去。

### 11

> Some have claimed that major glacier-fed rivers changed their course, dramatically affecting the water supply and agriculture; or that the cities could not cope with an increasing population, they exhausted their resource base, the trading economy broke down or they succumbed to invasion and conflict; and yet others that climate change caused an environmental change that affected food and water provision.

有人声称，主要的冰川补给河流改变了河道，从而严重影响供水和农业；也有人认为，城市无法应对不断增长的人口，资源基础被耗尽，贸易经济崩溃，或者城市屈服于入侵与冲突；还有人认为，气候变化造成环境改变，影响了食物和水的供应。

主干是 `Some have claimed that ...; or that ...; and yet others that ...`，三个 that 内容并列罗列假说；各分支内部又以分词或并列谓语展开。本句故意密集堆叠原因，随后作者会强调它们大多仍属推测。

本句词汇与短语：`claim`（B2）声称；`major`（B1）主要的；`glacier-fed`（C1，地理专业词）由冰川补给的；`change course`（B2）改变河道；`dramatically`（B2）显著地；`affect`（B1）影响；`water supply`（B1）供水；`agriculture`（B1）农业；`cope with`（B2）应对；`increasing`（B1）不断增长的；`population`（B1）人口；`exhaust`（C1）耗尽；`resource base`（C1）资源基础；`trading economy`（B2，经济专业表达）贸易经济；`break down`（B2）崩溃；`succumb to`（C2）屈服于；`invasion`（B2）入侵；`conflict`（B2）冲突；`climate change`（B1）气候变化；`environmental`（B1）环境的；`provision`（C1）供应；`cause`（B1）造成、引起。

**A层深挖 1｜`succumb to`（C2）**

本句中城市“屈服于入侵与冲突”，即抵抗失败并被压倒。`succumb` 常与 `to` 连用，既可指向诱惑或压力让步，也可指因疾病而死亡；Cambridge 将其核心义概括为失去抵抗决心、接受失败：[succumb｜Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/succumb)。

它比普通的 `lose` 更强调先有抵抗、后来撑不住；比 `give in to` 更正式，也更适合历史、疾病和制度压力语境。作者用它把“入侵和冲突”写成一种压倒城市体系的力量，但仍只是众多假说之一。可迁移使用：`The firm eventually succumbed to sustained financial pressure.` 公司最终在持续的财务压力下败下阵来。`She refused to succumb to the temptation to copy the answer.` 她拒绝向抄答案的诱惑屈服。近义表达 `yield to` 语气较中性，`surrender to` 带投降画面，`give in to` 更口语。

### 12

> ‘It is unlikely that there was a single cause for the decline of the civilisation.

“这一文明的衰落不太可能只有单一原因。

形式主语结构 `It is unlikely that ...` 表谨慎判断，that 从句是真正内容；`single cause` 否定一因论，为多因素研究定调。

本句词汇与短语：`be unlikely that`（B2）不太可能；`single cause`（B1）单一原因；`decline`（B2）衰落；`civilisation`（B2）文明。

### 13

> But the fact is, until now, we have had little solid evidence from the area for most of the key elements,’ said Petrie.

但事实是，直到现在，我们对大多数关键因素都几乎没有来自该地区的可靠证据。”Petrie说道。

主干是 `the fact is` 后接解释内容，`until now` 与现在完成时呼应；`little` 表示几乎没有。它把上一句的谨慎归因落实为证据不足。

本句词汇与短语：`the fact is`（B1）事实是；`until now`（B1）直到现在；`solid evidence`（B2）可靠证据；`area`（B1）地区；`key element`（B2）关键要素。

### 14

> ‘A lot of the archaeological debate has really only been well-argued speculation.’

“大量考古争论其实只是论证充分的推测。”

主干是 `debate has ... been speculation`；`well-argued` 承认论证质量，`only` 又限制其证据地位，形成有分寸的批评。

本句词汇与短语：`archaeological`（C1，考古学专业词）考古学的；`debate`（B2）争论；`well-argued`（C1）论证充分的；`speculation`（C1）推测。

### 15

> A research team led by Petrie, together with Dr Ravindanath Singh of Banaras Hindu University in India, found early in their investigations that many of the archaeological sites were not where they were supposed to be, completely altering understanding of the way that this region was inhabited in the past.

由Petrie带领、并与印度贝拿勒斯印度教大学的Ravindanath Singh博士合作的研究团队，在调查初期发现，许多考古遗址并不在先前认定的位置上，这彻底改变了人们对该地区过去居住方式的理解。

主干是 `A research team found that ...`；`led by ...` 和 `together with ...` 补充团队构成，where 从句作表语，`completely altering ...` 表结果，最后 that 从句修饰 way。长句把地点错误及其认知后果连在一起。

本句词汇与短语：`research team`（B1）研究团队；`lead`（B1）带领；`investigation`（B2）调查；`archaeological site`（C1，考古学专业表达）考古遗址；`be supposed to be`（B1）应当位于、被认为是；`completely`（B1）彻底地；`alter`（B2）改变；`understanding`（B1）理解；`region`（B1）地区；`inhabit`（C1）居住于。

### 16

> When they carried out a survey of how the larger area was settled in relation to sources of water, they found inaccuracies in the published geographic locations of ancient settlements ranging from several hundred metres to many kilometres.

当他们调查更大区域中的聚落如何相对于水源分布时，发现已公布的古代聚落地理位置存在误差，误差范围从数百米到数公里不等。

When 从句交代调查过程，其中 how 从句作 survey 的内容；主干是 `they found inaccuracies`，`ranging from ... to ...` 修饰误差幅度。数字说明旧数据并非小偏差。

本句词汇与短语：`carry out a survey`（B2）开展调查；`settle`（B2）定居、形成聚落；`in relation to`（B2）相对于、关于；`source of water`（B1）水源；`inaccuracy`（C1）错误、不准确；`published`（B1）已公布的；`geographic location`（B2，地理专业表达）地理位置；`ancient settlement`（C1，考古学专业表达）古代聚落；`range from ... to ...`（B2）范围从……到……；`metre`：基础词（A1/A2），米；`kilometre`：基础词（A1/A2），公里。

### 17

> They realised that any attempts to use the existing data were likely to be fundamentally flawed.

他们意识到，任何使用现有数据的尝试都很可能存在根本性缺陷。

主干是 `They realised that ...`；that 从句中 `attempts ... were likely to be flawed` 为核心，`to use ...` 修饰 attempts，`fundamentally` 把问题从局部误差升级为基础失效。

本句词汇与短语：`realise`（B1）意识到；`attempt`（B2）尝试；`existing data`（B2）现有数据；`be likely to`（B1）很可能；`fundamentally`（C1）根本上；`flawed`（C2）有缺陷的。

**A层深挖 2｜`fundamentally flawed`（C2）**

这里不是说数据“有一点不准”，而是说建立在这些坐标上的使用尝试可能从根基上就不可靠。`flawed` 指含有错误、弱点或缺陷；Cambridge 还直接给出 `fundamentally flawed` 这一商业英语搭配：[flawed｜Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/flawed)。

`fundamentally` 把缺陷落在基本前提或方法上，比 `slightly inaccurate` 严重得多；`wrong` 只给结论，`flawed` 则暗示推理、设计或数据过程存在结构性弱点。本句作用是解释团队为何必须重新实地调查。可迁移使用：`The forecast was fundamentally flawed because it relied on incomplete data.` 该预测依赖不完整数据，因而存在根本缺陷。`A fundamentally flawed admissions rule can reproduce inequality for years.` 一项存在根本缺陷的招生规则可能多年复制不平等。相近的 `faulty` 常指设备或推理出错，`defective` 多指产品有缺陷，`unsound` 常评价论证或财务基础不稳。

### 18

> Over the course of several seasons of fieldwork they carried out new surveys, finding an astonishing 198 settlement sites that were previously unknown.

在数个田野工作季期间，他们开展了新的调查，发现了令人惊讶的198处此前不为人知的聚落遗址。

主干是 `they carried out new surveys`；句首短语给时间跨度，`finding ...` 表调查结果，that 从句修饰 sites。新发现的数量证明重新测绘的价值。

本句词汇与短语：`over the course of`（B2）在……期间；`season`（B1）季节、工作季；`fieldwork`（C1，考古学专业词）田野调查；`carry out a survey`（B2）开展调查；`astonishing`（B2）令人惊讶的；`settlement site`（C1，考古学专业表达）聚落遗址；`previously`（B1）先前；`unknown`（B1）未知的。

### 19

> Now, research published by Dr Yama Dixit and Professor David Hodell, both from Cambridge’s Department of Earth Sciences, has provided the first definitive evidence for climate change affecting the plains of north-western India, where hundreds of Harappan sites are known to have been situated.

如今，剑桥大学地球科学系Yama Dixit博士和David Hodell教授发表的研究，首次为气候变化曾影响印度西北部平原提供了确凿证据；已知数百处哈拉帕遗址曾坐落于此。

主干是 `research has provided ... evidence`；过去分词短语说明研究发表者，同位语补充二人所属机构，where 从句修饰 plains。`first definitive evidence` 标志论证从推测进入实证。

本句词汇与短语：`publish`（B1）发表；`Department of Earth Sciences`（C1，地球科学专业表达）地球科学系；`provide evidence for`（B2）为……提供证据；`definitive`（C2）确凿的、决定性的；`climate change`（B1）气候变化；`affect`（B1）影响；`plain`（B2，地理专业词）平原；`north-western`（B1）西北部的；`Harappan site`（C1，考古学专业表达）哈拉帕遗址；`be situated`（B2）坐落于；`research`（B1）研究。

### 20

> The researchers gathered shells of Melanoides tuberculata snails from the sediments of an ancient lake and used geochemical analysis as a means of tracing the climate history of the region.

研究人员从一个古湖泊的沉积物中收集了瘤拟黑螺的贝壳，并以地球化学分析作为追踪该地区气候历史的手段。

主干以 `gathered` 和 `used` 并列呈现“取样—分析”；`as a means of` 说明方法用途。该句直接提供第32、33题答案。

本句词汇与短语：`researcher`（B1）研究人员；`gather`（B1）收集；`shell`（B1，生物学专业词）贝壳；`Melanoides tuberculata`（C2，生物学专业词）瘤拟黑螺；`snail`：基础词（A1/A2），蜗牛；`sediment`（C1，地质学专业词）沉积物；`ancient lake`（B1）古湖泊；`geochemical analysis`（C2，地球化学专业表达）地球化学分析；`as a means of`（B2）作为……的手段；`trace`（B2）追踪；`climate history`（B2，气候学专业表达）气候历史；`region`（B1）地区。

### 21

> ‘As today, the major source of water into the lake is likely to have been the summer monsoon,’ says Dixit.

“与今天一样，进入该湖泊的主要水源很可能是夏季季风。”Dixit说道。

引语主干是 `the major source ... is likely to have been ...`，完成不定式表示对过去的推断；`As today` 把古代水系与当代条件类比。

本句词汇与短语：`major`（B1）主要的；`source of water`（B1）水源；`lake`：基础词（A1/A2），湖泊；`be likely to have been`（B2）很可能曾经是；`summer monsoon`（C1，气候学专业表达）夏季季风。

### 22

> ‘But we have observed that there was an abrupt change about 4,100 years ago, when the amount of evaporation from the lake exceeded the rainfall – indicative of a drought.’

“但我们观察到，大约四千一百年前发生了一次突然变化：当时湖水蒸发量超过降雨量，这表明发生了干旱。”

### 23

### 24

> It has long been thought that other great Bronze Age civilisations also declined at a similar time, with a global-scale climate event being seen as the cause.

本句词汇与短语：`it has long been thought that`（B2）长期以来一直认为；`Bronze Age`（B2，考古学专业词）青铜时代；`civilisation`（B2）文明；`decline`（B2）衰落；`similar`（B1）相似的；`global-scale`（C1）全球尺度的；`climate event`（B2，气候学专业表达）气候事件；`be seen as`（B1）被视为；`cause`（B1）原因。

### 25

本句词汇与短语：`local-scale`（C1）地方尺度的；`process`（B2）过程；`be linked`（B2）相互关联；`archaeological`（C1，考古学专业词）考古学的；`interest lies in`（C1）意义在于；`impact on`（B2）对……的影响；`larger-scale`（C1）更大尺度的；`event`（B1）事件；`environment`（B1）环境；`population`（B1）人群。

本句词汇与短语：`considering`（B2）考虑到；`vast`（C1）辽阔的；`Harappan Civilisation`（C1，考古学专业表达）哈拉帕文明；`variable`（B2）多变的；`weather system`（B2，气候学专业表达）天气系统；`essential`（B2）至关重要的；`obtain`（B2）获取；`climate data`（B2，气候学专业表达）气候数据；`area`（B1）地区。

> Petrie and Singh’s team is now examining archaeological records and trying to understand details of how people led their lives in the region five millennia ago.

本句词汇与短语：`team`（B1）团队；`examine`（B2）审查；`archaeological record`（C1，考古学专业表达）考古记录；`try to understand`（B1）试图理解；`detail`（B1）细节；`lead one’s life`（B2）过某种生活；`region`（B1）地区；`millennium`（C1）一千年。

### 32

> ‘By investigating responses to environmental pressures and threats, we can learn from the past to engage with the public, and the relevant governmental and administrative bodies, to be more proactive in issues such as the management and administration of water supply, the balance of urban and rural development, and the importance of preserving cultural heritage in the future.’

本句词汇与短语：`investigate`（B2）研究；`response to`（B1）对……的反应；`environmental pressure`（B2）环境压力；`threat`（B2）威胁；`learn from the past`（B1）向过去学习；`engage with`（B2）与……沟通合作；`relevant`（B2）相关的；`governmental`（C1）政府的；`administrative body`（C1，公共管理专业表达）行政机构；`proactive`（C1）主动预防的；`issue`（B1）问题；`management`（B1）管理；`administration`（B2）行政管理；`water supply`（B1）供水；`balance`（B1）平衡；`urban`（B2）城市的；`rural`（B2）乡村的；`development`（B1）发展；`preserve`（B2）保护；`cultural heritage`（C1，文化遗产专业表达）文化遗产；`public`（B1）公众；`importance`（B1）重要性。

**A层深挖 3｜`be proactive`（C1）**

本句要求公共机构不要等水资源、城乡发展或遗产问题恶化后才反应，而要预先采取能带来改变的行动。`proactive` 与 `reactive` 相对；Cambridge 的释义正是主动促成改变，而非只在变化发生后回应：[proactive｜Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/proactive)。

它比普通的 `active` 多一层“提前预防或塑造结果”，常搭配 `approach / role / measures / management`。文章在结尾使用它，把考古研究变成面向未来的治理工具。可迁移使用：`Schools should be proactive about identifying learning difficulties.` 学校应主动及早识别学习困难。`A proactive maintenance plan can prevent costly failures.` 主动维护计划可以预防代价高昂的故障。近义的 `preventive` 强调预防具体危害，`forward-looking` 强调面向未来，`take the initiative` 则强调率先行动。

**官方答案：A（Cameron Petrie）。** H段第32句是Petrie的观点：研究过去的应对方式，可改善未来供水、发展和遗产治理。B要求更多地区数据；C、D讨论干旱证据与持续期。

#### 39

> Rough calculations indicate the approximate length of a period of water shortage.

粗略计算表明了一段缺水时期的大致持续时间。

**官方答案：D（David Hodell）。** E段第23句 `Hodell adds: We estimate ... lasted about 200 years`；estimate/about 对应 rough/approximate。逐项排除：A Petrie谈衰落证据不足和未来启示，没有估算缺水期长度；B Singh要求补充当地气候数据，没有给出时长；C Dixit说明蒸发超过降雨，却没有给出两百年估算。时长估算明确属于D Hodell。

#### 40

> Information about the decline of the Harappan Civilisation has been lacking.

关于哈拉帕文明衰落的信息一直不足。

**官方答案：A（Cameron Petrie）。** B段第4句Petrie说关于文明兴起证据很多，`but relatively little about its fall`，正是 decline information lacking 的同义改写。逐项排除：B Singh说需要更多当地气候数据，不是概括文明衰落信息长期不足；C Dixit报告湖泊证据及降雨—蒸发变化；D Hodell估算干旱时长。只有A Petrie直接作出“兴起资料多、衰落资料少”的对比。

文章最终没有宣布一个万能答案。它更重要的提醒是：地图坐标、湖泊贝壳、谷物和陶器分别照亮问题的一部分；只有把环境变化与不同群体的具体应对结合起来，考古学才能同时解释过去并帮助未来决策。
